Six disciplines.
One dojo.

From striking to grappling to the stick-fighting traditions of Tamil Nadu — train what fits you, or train it all.

Karate

Striking · Kata · Discipline

Traditional striking, stances and kata — the foundation most students begin with.

Judo

Throws · Grappling · Control

Balance, leverage and throws — using an opponent's force against them.

Kudo

Full Contact · Headgear

A hybrid full-contact style blending striking and grappling under protective gear.

Silambam

Tamil Weapon Art · Bamboo Staff

Tamil Nadu's own martial art — footwork and staff work passed down for centuries.

Kickboxing

Conditioning · Combos

Fast-paced striking combinations built for fitness, speed and stamina.

Muay Thai

The Art of Eight Limbs

Fists, elbows, knees and shins — Thailand's striking art, taught with respect for its roots.

Warriors Combat Fitness runs out of P.N. Road in Tirupur, training students across six martial arts and combat sports under one roof — from traditional Karate and Judo to Tamil Nadu's own Silambam, alongside Kudo, Kickboxing and Muay Thai.

Classes run every evening, Monday through Saturday, for children and adults alike. Whether the goal is self-defense, competition, or simply fitness with structure, students train at their own level while working toward the next belt.
